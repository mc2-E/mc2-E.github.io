## Research themes

### Materials in extreme environments

We study how interacting thermal, mechanical, chemical, and irradiation conditions influence material behavior. Coupled models connect microstructural evolution with changes in properties and performance.

### Multiphysics and multiscale modeling

Our methods span atomistic, phase-field, and finite-element descriptions. We use these complementary approaches to examine mechanisms across length scales and connect local material behavior to larger-scale response.

### AI/ML and reduced-order models

We develop efficient representations of materials properties and performance to support parameter studies and design. These approaches connect physics-based calculations and available data, with attention to validation and uncertainty.

### Nuclear fuels, claddings, and structural materials

Our work addresses materials for advanced nuclear reactors, including the evolution and performance of fuels, claddings, and structural alloys.

### Fusion and battery materials

We also apply computational materials methods to fusion-energy and battery-material challenges, examining how material structure and coupled physical processes affect performance.
