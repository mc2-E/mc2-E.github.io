The retained mathematical model

Composition is q=(xNi,xCr,xv). Initially it equals the fixed far composition. Increasing the vacancy reference preserves occupied-site Fe:Ni:Cr ratios. The thermal vacancy normalization remains fixed.

Sv=xv,bxv,thermal,mi=Di*xi,bSvRT,D=BH

The full Hessian H and linked mobility B are recalculated for each selected reference, then frozen during transport. In PTK, the Cr input changes both Cr transport and its vacancy-exchange terms. PT keeps only the diagonal of B; it still retains thermodynamic coupling. D* is a tracer input; D is the coupled chemical diffusion matrix.

∂q∂t=D∂2q∂x2,D∂q∂x0=sr,s=(0,1,−1)T

Positive r removes Cr and creates vacancies. With molar site density Csite, the current density is j = 2 F Csite r (r in m/s). The integrated removal is the time integral of r.

Mixed potential: finite bath or maintained chemistry

The anodic and cathodic currents balance at every time. A and V are alloy Cr and vacancy activity ratios relative to the retained thermal reference; H, P and S are the normalized salt reactant/product activities.

ECr=VT2ln(VS/A)EH=0.45 V+VT[lnH−12lnP]j=2j0CrAsinh[(E−ECr)/VT]j=−2j0HHPsinh[(E−EH)/(2VT)]

Maintained chemistry: H = P = S = 1. Finite bath: H = 1 − u, P = 1 + u, S = 1 + 10u, with du/dt = Csiter/Cbath. Cbath = 1.88507 mol/m² and Csite = 10⁵ mol/m³; both exchange-current scales are 5 A/m². These are retained diagnostic settings, not a calibration to a specific operating plant.

Final does not mean two years

Finite-bath equilibrium has zero net current and a finite accumulated removal. Its alloy profile becomes uniform at the far composition. Maintained chemistry and positive prescribed overpotential retain a steady gradient and current; their accumulated removal continues to increase. A changed D* can change the approach time without changing the finite-bath terminal amount.

How predictions are calculated

Bulk diffusion uses the existing analytical kernels. Prescribed-potential histories use Laplace inversion. Mixed-potential histories use those kernels with numerical boundary-current balance and piecewise-linear history quadrature: a semi-analytical calculation. All coefficient, activity and bath definitions are retained from the earlier explorers. No new MOOSE results are included.
