What is being inverted?

The bulk operator is evaluated at the chosen initial/far composition and then frozen. PT retains the full thermodynamic Hessian H with diagonal mobility B. PTK retains the full H and B, including metal–vacancy kinetic coupling.

D=BH,a=D−1s,qs=qb−Lar

Here q = (xNi, xCr, xv), s = (0, 1, −1), and Fe fills the remaining sites. Positive r removes Cr and injects an equal number of vacancies. The steady profiles are q(x) = qb − (L − x)ar, with the exposed surface at x = 0 and the fixed alloy reservoir at x = L.

Prescribed overpotential: exact algebraic inversion

This branch retains the earlier analytical linear concentration surface law. It is different from the full MOOSE model’s nonlinear activity law.

r=hxCr,s,rss=hxCr,b1+hLaCr

h = j0sinh(η/VT)/(F CsxCr,b). For a target rt, calculate xCr,s = xCr,b − L aCrrt, then η = VTasinh[rtF CsxCr,b/(j0xCr,s)]. If that surface Cr value is zero or negative, no finite η can reach the target. The transport ceiling is xCr,b/(L aCr).

Maintained chemistry: solve current balance

The bath keeps its normalized HF, H₂ and dissolved-Cr activities at 1. Its equilibrium-potential gap is 0.45 V. The surface Cr and vacancy activities A and V retain their full nonlinear expressions and fixed thermal normalization.

ECr=VT2ln(V/A),j=2FCsr

The potential must satisfy both half-reactions: E = ECr + VTasinh[j/(2j0,CrA)] = 0.45 − 2VTasinh[j/(2j0,H)]. The spatial solution is analytical; this nonlinear scalar equality is solved numerically. E and ηCr are outputs. The finite-bath terminal equilibrium has zero net corrosion and cannot supply a positive steady-state target.

What changes when a parameter is varied?

  • Vacancies: vb = S vthermal; occupied-site Fe:Ni:Cr ratios are preserved. Both H and B are reevaluated for every trial S, with the original thermal normalization unchanged.
  • Cr tracer input: only D*Cr is varied; Fe/Ni inputs stay fixed. All linked Cr–vacancy mobility terms and Bvv are rebuilt before calculating D = BH. D*Cr is not the matrix entry DCrCr. The reference-state vacancy-scaled tracer estimate is S D*Cr.
  • Overpotential: η changes the prescribed reaction coefficient, while bulk coefficients stay fixed. Increasing η cannot exceed the transport ceiling.
Interpretation. These are inverse benchmark requirements, not fitted material properties or predictions from the full nonlinear MOOSE model. A specified supersaturation requires a physical mechanism to maintain it. Search windows are S = 1–10,000, η = 0–1 V, and Cr tracer multiplier = 10⁻⁴–10⁶. The diffusivity window is a search setting, not a literature range. The prescribed and mixed branches retain their distinct earlier interface laws; their difference is not solely a bath comparison.

Numerical reference

Retained exchange-current inputs: j0,Cr = j0,H = 5 A/m². These are model inputs, not experimentally calibrated values. No voids, interface motion, or elapsed-time claim is added. Reaching a steady-state rate does not specify when that rate is approached.
